Feature spec: Digital Wallet Transfer Limits & Account Tiers
A mobile wallet app lets users send money to other wallet users and to bank accounts. Every account belongs
to one of three tiers, based on the level of identity verification (KYC) completed.
1. Tier 1 accounts may send up to 20,000 per transaction and 50,000 per day. Tier 2: 200,000 per transaction,
500,000 per day. Tier 3: no per-transaction limit, 5,000,000 per day.
2. The daily limit resets at midnight.
3. Pending transfers count toward the daily limit. If a transfer fails or is reversed, the amount is returned to
the user's available daily limit.
4. Transfers to a user's own linked bank accounts are exempt from all limits.
5. Users can schedule transfers for a future date; scheduled transfers execute at 8:00 AM on the scheduled
day.
6. When a user completes the next KYC level, the tier upgrade takes effect immediately.
7. If a transfer would exceed the remaining daily limit, it is rejected with a clear error message showing the
remaining limit.
